# Welcome to SHADOWCRYSTAL ENGINEERING

### FRC TEAM 11755 - MARYLAND - MONTGOMERY & FREDERICK COUNTIES
A student-led FIRST robotics team and non-profit STEM organization based in Germantown, Maryland. Our CASTLE TOWN (home base) is located at Seneca Valley High School at Crystal Rock Drive in Germantown.
>Recruitment is now open to all high school students and prospective mentors in Montgomery and Frederick Counties! No prior experience is required!
---
## TEAM OVERVIEW
- 4 student-led subteams: Mechanical, Electrical, Software, and Business.
- 12 student members and counting.
- 5 mentors/advisors (subject matter experts)
- Members from 5 high schools across the world 
---
## PROGRAMS
- FIRST Robotics Competition Team 11755
- STEMinars
- Montgomery County Robotics Coalition (CoRoCo)

## WHO WE SERVE
Open to any high school students (grade 9-12) from any school regardless of experience, background, or finanical status. We also perform outreach events for younger students in order to spark passions for STEM. 

### NO PRIOR EXPERIENCE REQUIRED
Everyone starts somewhere. Our mentors and student leaders provide guidance and education. No matter what, you will succeed if you bring your total focus to our meetings.
